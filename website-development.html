The homepage blueprint

It all rides on your homepage.

Your homepage is the only page most people will ever read. Get it right and the phone rings. Get it wrong and the best business in town stays invisible.

That’s why it’s the first page we design. Nothing on it is decoration. Here’s the job each block does to turn a stranger into an enquiry.

  1. 01Nav + tap-to-call

    First thing they see, first decision they make. A clear logo, a couple of links and your phone number front and centre says “you’re in the right place”, and lets the ready-to-buy call before they even scroll.

  2. 02Hero section

    You’ve got a few seconds to answer “can these people help me?” A plain headline, your trade plus your town, says yes. One obvious button kills the “what now?” It’s the shortest path from stranger to lead.

  3. 03Services

    People scan for their exact problem. Seeing it named back to them (blocked drains, switchboard upgrades, whatever you do) flips a browser into “oh, they do my thing.” And Google ranks you for each one.

  4. 04Why us

    Every business claims “quality and reliable.” This answers the question they’re actually asking, “why you over the other five quotes?”, and gives them a reason not to just pick the cheapest.

  5. ★★★
    ★★★
    05Social proof

    Nobody believes what you say about yourself. They believe what other people say about you. Real reviews with real names and suburbs kill the “are these guys legit?” doubt on the spot.

  6. 06Photo gallery

    Trades are visual and trust-heavy. Seeing your actual finished work makes it real and takes the risk out of choosing you. It’s the single biggest thing that turns a “maybe” into a call.

  7. 07FAQ

    Every unanswered worry is a reason to close the tab. Handling the common objections up front (price, timing, mess, guarantees) keeps them moving toward the enquiry. Google loves it too.

  8. 08Contact form

    Not everyone wants to call, especially after hours or mid-job. A dead-simple form (name, phone, message) catches the quiet leads you’d otherwise never hear from.

  9. 09Floating call button

    The urge to act can strike on any section. A call button that follows them down the page means they never scroll back to find your number. You catch the impulse the second it hits.
